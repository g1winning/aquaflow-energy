Investor enquiries

Privacy

What happens to the details you send us. November 2026.

What we collect

The name, email address, organisation and message you type into the investor enquiry form, and the options you tick on it. We also record the address of the page the form was sent from.

How it reaches us

The form is sent by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service, which passes it to AquaFlow Energy by email.

What we use it for

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to send the investor documents you ask for. We do not sell them and we do not share them for marketing.

Other services

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and some documents open on Google Drive. Google may log your IP address when you use them.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

See or delete your details

Email geoffrey@aquaflowenergy.com to see or delete the details you have sent us.
