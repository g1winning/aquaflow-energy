AboutAquaFlow

Aqua Flow Energy makes turbines that make low-cost electricity 24/7 from moving water.

The turbines sit below the surface in rivers, tidal channels, ocean currents and large water pipes.

Solar and wind produce power 30% of the time depending on the weather and time of day. A turbine in moving water will supply power around the clock without a battery bank.

Units are 2 to 10 metres across and fit in a shipping container. They are built from stock industrial components with one moving part, and use no rare earth metals.

The First Tranche of US$2 million pays for full-scale prototypes and field trials in Melbourne and the USA. The Second Tranche of US$20 million funds a US factory and licensed partner builds after positive trials.

Unit sizes and blade types

Units run from 2 to 10 metres across. Blade shape is matched to the local water speed, from 0.5 to 5 metres per second.

The range includes two and three blade rotors, spiral rotors, and an Archimedes spiral built for slow, open flow. There are six core models in design.

Parts and materials

Generators and drivetrains are adapted from proven technology and natural shapes and most parts are bought off the shelf. The shaft is the main moving part, and in some units a gearbox will be used to increase RPM.

Most of the generator unit is iron and copper. The frame is stainless steel and recycled plastic for blades.

Shipping and installation

Units fit standard ISO shipping containers and can be assembled on site in about four hours. No oversize freight permits or heavy-lift ships are needed.

An installation barge carries two or three unit types. The crew measures flow speed and direction at the spot and lowers the unit that suits it.

Servicing

The generator sits in a pod that lifts out. A fresh pod goes in and the old one is serviced on land.

Units work at 5 to 20 metres depth, within reach of divers and a standard workboat. The planned service interval is six months. The rest of an array keeps running while one unit is serviced.

Fish safety

Spiral blades turn slowly and leave open space for fish to pass. Faster units sit behind screens and ducts that keep fish out of the blade zone and smooth the flow into the rotor.

We will measure the effect on fish and marine mammals during field trials.

Data centre case

Diesel replacement

Island communities

Many islands run on imported diesel, and solar needs large batteries to cover the night. Units at several tidal points around an island cover each other’s slack water, so the array runs all day and night. Local crews with a workboat can service it.

Economic statement

Yarra and other city rivers

Cities on rivers

Most large cities sit on a river or a bay. Units hang below bridges and walkways close to the people using the power, which cuts the need for new transmission lines. Melbourne on the Yarra is the first target.

Shared foundations and cables

Offshore wind farms

Wind farm foundations already stand in deep water with grid cables attached. Adding water turbines to them gives a wind farm steady output when the wind drops, at a lower cost than building a new site. The engineering review names this as a major opportunity.

Unlike solar or wind, hydrokinetic energy remains stable at night, during storms, and in winter, making it a valuable complement to existing renewable portfolios.
  1. Unlike solar or wind, hydrokinetic energy remains stable at night, during storms, and in winter, making it a valuable complement to existing renewable portfolios.
  2. Australia possesses extensive untapped water resources that flow continually, predictably, and in proximity to population centres.
  3. The most investable opportunities are those that pair a verified flow resource with turbine architectures engineered for reliability, simplified maintenance, and ecological safety.

Why we are doing this

Clean power that is practical, efficient and profitable. The river runs whether or not the sun shines, and every unit we put in it is cash and evidence at once.

Practical

Stock parts, a shipping container and a workboat. No dam, no new transmission line, no rare earths.

Efficient

Water is 800 times denser than air. A small rotor in a steady current does the work of a large one in the wind.

Profitable

Units are sold with a service contract from day one. The plan returns investors' cash by around the thirty-sixth month in the Base case.
